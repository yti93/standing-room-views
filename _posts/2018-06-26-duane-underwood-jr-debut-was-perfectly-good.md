Bottom 1 –

Two on, two out. Matt Kemp up. 1-2, to 3-2 with balls away and down. Fouled off, challenged him 94 away which he fouled off, after which he fouled a ball in on the hands. I liked Chris Gimenez's mound visit to calm him down and talk about the next couple pitches. Kemp's fouled off 3 straight. 34 pitches on the night already. Taking a lot of time in between pitches. Good mix of fastballs and breaking pitches, all of which Kemp has been spoiling. Went up and in to Kemp and he draws the walk. Underwood really laboring here, Hickey out to visit with the bases loaded.

How does he do with Grandal? A very deep, very unproductive out to Almora and Underwood escapes unscathed, and 41 pitches into his Major League career.

Bottom 2 –

Kike crushes one, grooved. Two pitches in and he looks like he's not hitting his spots. That fastball ran right back into Hernandez's wheelhouse. After that, calmed down. Puig didn't take the bat off the shoulder until a 2-0 fastball was grooved down the middle. 2 weak groundouts and a broken bat flyout to Heyward later, a relatively quiet inning.

Bottom 3 –

4 pitch walk, quick double play and Underwood. Drops a beauty of a curve in on Bellinger for strike 1, and gets him on a disappearing fastball for strike 3. 2nd and 3rd innings have been relatively quick, which has helped reset his pitch count somewhat, and makes five innings a possibility.

Bottom 4 –

Changeup looks good as Matt Kemp hits it 10 feet. Again drops a strike on the hook in against a left handed batter, this time to Grandal. Four pitches, two outs. Full count, strike 3 on Kike Hernandez. Underwood only with two hits allowed and one earned run.

Overall, an abbreviated, but impressive debut. 41 pitches in the first, and buckled down with only a dinger to Hernandez blemishing his line over 4 impressive innings. I thought he settled in well and pitched through trouble admirably. Kudos on your Major League debut, Duane.
