One of my friends that I reconnected with earlier this month was telling me about Zach Cregger's "Elf Draft" or "Troll Draft" mentality ([link here](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DaJFyOKuLxp/?hl=en)) when writing just to put something on the page, and I felt drawn to it so this is one of my attempts this week just to write down something. Note this is not a screenplay.

The premise of this is to just write like you're a dumb elf or troll and you're putting a first draft down that you can then go back and revisit and edit with a more discerning eye. As someone who is deeply critical of themselves when thinking about how journal better, I felt like this could work for me. Anyway, I dream of opening a bookstore and below is my Elf/Troll Draft.

I want to open a bookstore. It's not a big bookstore, and not a big-box bookstore. It's got recommendations written by hand, by the people that work there. It's a third space and third place for people.

It has a fun name, a name that means something. A name that means something to me as well. I don't have the name yet. Maybe the name means something to the community (like Left Bank Books in Seattle).

It's not just a bookstore but a true third space. It can appeal to people of all ages, and I want it to. It has great coffee and a little section for music.

Maybe it has a woodworking area and a little garden in the back, where our kids can play.

And the books! - It has books for bookworms, & books for people that don't like books. It hopefully has a good manga and comic collection, and spaces for book clubs and gatherings.

It's a place where parents can bring their children and feel safe, and the children feel safe and feel a sense of wonder. We'll have book clubs and gatherings that anyone can join, we will have new books and used books, and we will have all the books you loved growing up.

Maybe we'll have a coworking space but that feels like an overreach or a bit of a stretch. It will be with the times and tech-forward, but certainly not tech-oriented or tech-obsessed. Tech-cognizant, maybe. I still want it to look and feel manual, old-fashioned, analog, and slow in all the best ways.

We'll be friends with other booksellers in the area and be loving and welcoming. But also stand up for what we believe is right and fair and just. There is no room for hate.

Our bookstore will have book art, and all the books I love and maybe some of the books I don't love.

But this bookstore, this dream, this hope, is going to be an extension and representation of who I am and who I want to be.
