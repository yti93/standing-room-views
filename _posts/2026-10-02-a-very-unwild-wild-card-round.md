I've got some baseball thoughts about the wild card round that just wrapped up over the course of ~50 hours from Tuesday to Thursday.

First of all - I'm just not even sure how people on the East Coast watch any sort of late night sports. I'm West Coast spoiled, and getting games starting before noon and ending before midnight is truly an embarrassment of riches. 

I say this because I stayed up until 1am Tuesday (Wednesday) and Wednesday (Thursday) watching my Cubbies get steamrolled by the fall juggernaut that has been the San Diego Padres. 

My saltiness about East Coast sports watching was, and is, exacerbated because the streaky Cubs, by definition of two 10-game winning streaks and one 10-game losing streak this year, decided to streak into the offseason with one measly extra base hit over 18 innings. If I'm being honest, the series was over the moment Tatis launched the first pitch the Padres saw this series into orbit. What a crowd for San Diego.

While we were on the East Coast, we had tickets to catch the Cubs and Red Sox at Fenway during the final weekend of the regular season. This was meant to be the start of our lovely leaf peepin' trip up and down New England, however the weather did not cooperate. The Nor'Easter ensured our Fenway plans went south as the storm followed us around the Eastern Seaboard this week. 

However bad the storms were this week, Red Sox fans probably felt worse incessantly hearing how a couple of born-and-bred Massholes were now employed by the Evil Empire. Not just employed by the Yanks, but gleefully bashing homers and retiring dudes up and down the order and trolling on twitter. Like the Cubs-Dads series, this one was over before it started.

I'm absolutely rooting for the White Sox going forward. Nothing that hasn't been repeated ad nauseam already by the talking heads, but going from 100+ losses a year to winning a series in the playoffs on the road is quite impressive. Not trying to talk trash, but it's a no-name roster compared to the star power left in the playoffs and that's what makes them fun. Throw out the regular season records, this team will be in the ALCS (by bracketology luck as much as skill, but hey that's further than 26 other teams). Astros need to blow it up (although Crane is trying his hardest).

And finally, the one series that went the distance also may have been the most predictable to predict before the series started. The Braves have been a damn solid team this year, and the Phillies worked their senior citizens to the bone this year. Watching Harper this series was agonizing - dude was completely unplugged. 

This all brings me to my original point - I saw someone suggest that all but one of the best of 3 Wild Card Series resulting in the Game 1 winner advancing means that we should go back to a winner-take-all Wild Card Round, and honestly I don't hate it. All hands on deck, energy would be insane, and teams get less of an advantage in the Divisional Series (although Wild Card teams have had a pretty good success rate in the Divisional Round, further proving that rest vs. rust is completely arbitrary).

Anyway, predictions for the DS series:

**NL**

- Brewers 3-1 Padres
- Dodgers 3-2 Braves

**AL**

- Yankees 3-1 Rays
- White Sox 3-2 Guardians
