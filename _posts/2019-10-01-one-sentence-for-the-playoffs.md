To keep up with blogging, I'm going to predict the playoffs and re-evaluate after every round. These are quick hits people.

AL Wild Card

Rays @ Athletics (Charlie Morton vs Sean Manaea): This could very well turn into a bullpen game if the A's get going early against Morton, but I like the Rays to manufacture a lead before they run into the latest formerly ho-hum reliever with a career year for the books Hendriks - the key here for the Rays (this will be a prevalent theme) is going to be limiting the long ball (they're great at that, with a 2nd-best in baseball HR/FB rate of 13.4%...behind the Athletics) - I'll take the Rays behind Morton's pitching.

NL Wild Card

Brewers @ Nationals (Brandon Woodruff vs Max Scherzer): This is a tough one, because on paper, the Nationals look to have a better offense with arguably the best pitcher in baseball (although not in the 2nd half) on the bump at home - but the Brewers under Counsell have reeled off another red-hot September run and look to have a little bit of that Devil Magic their NL Central playoff compatriots have demonstrated in spades over the past decade - this one is even more of a toss-up than the ALWC - but the Brewers' luck runs out here as Scherzer deals, the Brewers falter on offense (only the Cardinals have a worse WRC+ among playoff teams than the Brewers' 97) and the Nats take it (I lean on taking the best starter in these playoffs which we know never bodes well).

ALDS

Twins vs Yankees: Dingers galore - full write-up after the Wild Card games incoming, but you don't need me to tell you that we could see an average of 5 homers a game in this series - which means the Twins and Yanks are definitely going to combine for pitchers duels galore - Yanks in 4.

Rays vs Astros: There were times this year where the Rays looked like the 4th best team in baseball, and it's not a stretch to think they could surprise the 'Stros by doing everything well - only to steal a game or two - Astros in 4.

NLDS

Cardinals vs Braves: The Braves have been the best team no one's taking seriously this year, led by the next best player in baseball at the ripe old age of 22, with key bullpen pickups anchoring one of the most dynamic lineup in the majors - the Cardinals make it interesting with their pitching and a couple of Devil Magic hits, but the Braves persevere here in 5.

Nationals vs Dodgers: It's almost a given that the Dodgers will make their 4th consecutive NLCS - I say almost, because the Nationals have a formidable starting staff that could make life difficult for the Chavez Ravine sluggers, along with a guy that had every bit a season to remember as Bellinger did - the Dodgers, however, have those young guns in the 'pen and sparkplugs galore on the bench, and overwhelm the Nats here in 4 games.

ALCS

Yankees vs Astros: Brief sentences for the Championship Series - Yankees hit, Astros hit, Astros pitch, Yankees don't - Astros in 6.

NLCS

Braves vs Dodgers: The upset of the postseason here for me as the Dodgers run into some great pitching and my hunch that the Braves offense will be the story of October - Dodgers' magic runs out as the Braves take it in 7.

World Series

Braves vs Astros: Way too early to call this one, but no one's beating the Astros in my eyes - most pitching strikeouts and fewest batting strikeouts is a lethal combo in October, and experience wins out - Astros in 6.
