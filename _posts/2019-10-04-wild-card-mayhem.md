I felt pretty good about going 2-2 in my Wild Card predictions (editors note: written before the Cardinals and Dodgers won their respective games last night), so let's see how our Division Series matchups and predictions stack up.

NLDS (Written after game 1):

Cardinals @ Braves: I feel less confident about the Braves than I did 3 days ago - admittedly haven't watched their bullpen too much this season, but giving up 6 runs in 2 innings is worrisome for the rest of October, much less this series - the Cardinals bats haven't missed a beat since September - I'll still go Braves, but in 5 this go-around.

Nationals @ Dodgers: The well-oiled machine that is the NLDS Dodgers hummed right along as they thumped a familiar October adversary last night - Buehler looked fired up, the offense broke it open late, and they're one more win closer to the World Series return they've been dreaming of - Dodgers in 4, unrevised.

ALDS:

Twins @ Yankees: Prediction stays intact as the Bronx Bombers dispatch the Bomba Squad in this series - New York's bullpen seems a little more built to handle the onslaught of uppercuts the bats will unleash upon those poor baseballs at Target Field and Yankee Stadium - Yanks in 4.

Rays @ Astros: The Juice Series looks a little more intriguing with how thoroughly the Rays dismantled a potent Oakland offense, but the Astros' team .848 OPS is another species of October monster - we know how the Astros' lineup is balanced, full of patient hitters and fearsome power, but I've reevaluated and I'll go out on a limb and say the Rays' Big 3 has a very real chance of outpitching the Astros' trio - at least once through the rotation in October (still hard to pick the Tropicana boys though) - Astros in 5.

My CS and World Series picks remain unchanged - Astros over Braves in 6 - the Braves offense looks every bit as promised, but that bullpen better right itself before they pitch themselves into a Bobby Cox NLDS Exit Special.
