Well well well, the baseball gods smiled on us this year and gave us what we dream about during the dog days of August - a total of 5(!) win-or-go-home games within the first round (and a half) of the playoffs. And yes, I do count the Wild Card games as the playoffs - I mean, did you see how excited D.C. was about Juan Soto last week? He's 3-14 in the NLDS but the shine of a postseason hero takes a lot longer to wear off.

Anyway, this is going to be a quick hit before I recap both Division Series and preview the NLCS and ALCS this weekend, but I wanted to shout out a couple of things I loved over the first week of the playoffs and throw a quick game 5 prediction for each of the 3 do-or-die games.

- The Rays shut down the Astros by...starting Diego Castillo and closing with Blake Snell? The Rays had the gumption to bullpen game 4 in a do-or-die against the presumptive Cy Young winner, and dominated the game from start to finish. They are, to paraphrase Tommy Pham, believing in themselves.

- I'm here for the ovation D.C. gave their hometown hero, Ryan Zimmerman, as he hit a game 4 four-bagger. For someone that's not part of the new wave of Natitude, that moment probably meant a lot to Zim and hopefully showed him he still has it.

- The Yankees could toss Henry Rowengartner out on the mound and still have a chance to win with that offense. They might be the new favorites for now.

- Would you rather have your best player mess up somewhat egregiously and go 3-4 otherwise, or go 0-4 playing the game "the right way"? That's the case with Mr. Acuña, and without him the Braves would already be on the couch at home. He's a sparkplug and he's going to learn from his mistakes. Sitting him would be foolish beyond comprehension.

- We are one game away from the prohibitive favorites being eliminated before their respective Championship Series. In a season where it seemed like the Astros, Dodgers, Yankees, and everyone else - everyone else is making noise. I could see any one of the 7 teams left winning it all - but I want mayhem.

- What's the Dodgers' deal? Young talent, deep bench and staff, superior-ish bullpen - and they can't put away a Nationals team that lost their "best" player over the offseason and with an ace that hasn't been himself since the All-Star Break? The Nats look hungrier, but you might imply that the Dodgers are feeling a little more pressure with this current nucleus.

- Apparently, bat flips against the Cardinals are disrespectful, but Yadi throwing his bat almost as far as his game-winning sac fly is playing the game "the right way". I hope the Braves beat the brakes off of the Cardinals and their "Best Fans in Baseball". Yes I'm salty.

- I don't think anyone counted the Astros out yesterday until the final play of the game, which is why I feel like tomorrow's game 5 is shaping up to be a classic. We'll see how much mettle the Astros really possess.

- The Twins defied expectations for some this year, but that takes away from the excellent job the front office has done almost on the fly. After bottoming out in 2016 with 103 losses, they almost reversed it in 2019 with 101 wins and did so with smart, under-the-radar signings of veterans combined with a propensity to mash home runs. It stings seeing some of the relievers that got away have success for playoff teams (hi Ryan Pressly and Nick Anderson), but this Twins team is hungry, power-happy, and smartly run - a great formula for today's baseball environment.

Predictions

Braves over Cardinals

Nationals over Dodgers

Rays over Astros **

** This one is a toss up, and I'm hoping it goes 18, with Snell coming on to pitch the 15th, 16th, and 17th innings before Adames hits his 2nd homer of the game and 4th of the postseason in his coming out party to win it for those Devil (Magic) Rays.
