Hey guys. Youssef here. I'll be brief - I'm here to talk any and all things baseball. Whether it's a deep dive into conventional statistics to debating the merits of the newest flavor in sabermetrics, or discussing the ideal walk-up song, I'm here to entertain, to answer your questions, and to occasionally share some personal anecdotes. My goals are to educate and explain the conversation around baseball, however complicated it may seem, in everyman's terms.

I'm doing this to work on a couple of things - my writing skills, my baseball analysis, and conquering my fear of putting out content that won't be well-received. I'm hoping that my readers, whoever you are, are willing to go on this journey with me and provide honest feedback.
